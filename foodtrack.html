Eat Healthy

FoodTrack: The Smart Path to Personalized Health

FoodTrack is an intelligent calorie-tracking app that helps users easily manage their diet and health. By combining advanced AI technologies with traditional Chinese medicine wisdom and modern Western nutrition science, it transforms the dietary experience, offering a comprehensive and personalized health management solution. It eliminates the pain point of tedious manual data entry.

AI recognition

Effortless AI Food, Tongue, and Facial Recognition

FoodTrack uses AI and advanced machine learning algorithms to automatically identify food from photos and calculate nutritional values. With just one snapshot, users receive instant nutrition data—no manual input required.

Health profiles

Comprehensive Health Profiles & Personalized Advice

By analyzing tongue and facial photos alongside responses to the traditional “Ten Questions” of Chinese medicine, the app generates detailed health profiles and tailored dietary advice. It precisely evaluates calories, proteins, fats, carbohydrates, cholesterol, purine, and sugar for accurate daily intake management.

Motivation

Gamified Interactive Experience

A virtual pet bear represents the user, reflecting their health status while offering playful, gamified reminders to stay on track. This 3D companion turns abstract health data into an engaging visual journey, boosting long-term motivation and adherence.

Chronic condition support

Dedicated Support for Hypertension, Diabetes, and Hyperlipidemia

FoodTrack leverages AI and data visualization to monitor blood glucose, cholesterol, and blood pressure. With real-time alerts, trend analysis, and personalized suggestions, users can clearly understand risks while integrating weight goals, nutrition intake, and exercise data—building a sustainable, closed-loop health management system.

Ecosystem

One-Stop Healthy Living Experience

From health checks to meal recommendations, FoodTrack creates a seamless consumer journey. The system intelligently suggests functional soups and meals tailored to users’ body constitutions, presenting their nutritional benefits clearly. From detection and recommendation to ordering and feedback, FoodTrack builds a complete health ecosystem—making every choice smarter and healthier.
