Case Study

Learning & Evaluation Management System (LEMS)

Bay Technologies partnered with a large public primary school in Southern China to build a cloud-native Learning & Evaluation Management System that puts China's education evaluation reform policy, "Deepening Reform of Education Evaluation in the New Era," into daily teacher practice — serving about 2,900 students and 120+ teachers. The system replaces score-centric assessment with a "5 + 2" whole-child model (moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic, labor) and records process evidence and outcomes in longitudinal learner profiles.

Running on Spring Cloud microservices with strict data governance, the platform unifies course, assessment, behavior, arts/sports/labor, counseling, and family-engagement data — changing classroom practice while delivering measurable operational efficiency.

At a Glance

Client

A large public primary school in Southern China

Scale

~2,900 students, 120+ teachers, 57 classes

Context

Full-campus wired and Wi-Fi coverage, interactive displays in every classroom

The Challenge

Policy calls for a shift from score-only assessment to whole-person development (moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic, labor — 德智體美勞). The school needed to:

  • Unify fragmented spreadsheets and one-off tools into a single longitudinal profile.
  • Capture process evidence alongside outcomes, not just final scores.
  • Involve students, parents, teachers, and community in a traceable 360° evaluation.
  • Standardize data for municipal reporting and cross-school exchange with privacy protected.
  • Motivate participation through campus-wide programs.
Our approach

The "5 + 2" Evaluation Model

Co-designed with education authorities and the school's expert committee: five domains — Moral, Intellectual, Physical, Aesthetic, Labor — observed through two lenses, Process (behaviors, participation, artifacts, reflections) and Outcome (summative results, certifications, competitions) — giving a vertical and horizontal view of every learner.

Solution

What We Delivered

Holistic Evaluation & e-Portfolios

Rubrics, evidence uploads, annotations, and self/peer/parent inputs.

Course & Assessment Management

Participation tracking, formative checks, standards-aligned exams, multi-subject reports.

Character & Well-being Modules

Conduct logs, behavior recognition, role-based counseling notes, support plans.

Arts, Sports & Labor Education

Skill progression, scheduling, reflective journals, community-service capture.

"Talent Arena" (Challenge & Defend)

Campus-wide showcases and skill challenges that build culture and motivation.

Data Governance & Reporting

Unified data model, export services, cross-school exchange.

Delivery

Implementation & Change Management

  1. Policy to rubricsTeacher-friendly rubrics and weightings designed per grade band.
  2. Iterative roll-outPilots in selected grades, then full-school adoption.
  3. Capability buildingTraining, exemplar libraries, and ongoing "office hours" for teachers.
  4. GovernanceA school-level data committee overseeing indicators, privacy, and the release calendar.
