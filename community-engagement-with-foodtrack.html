Case Study

Driving Community Engagement & Clinic Relationships with FoodTrack

Client types

Community organizations and Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) clinics

Use case

Ongoing engagement and relationship management, beyond one-off events

Solution

FoodTrack tongue-scan nutrition & TCM insights, personalized nudges, and a lightweight CRM

Channels

Mini web-app with QR onboarding at events and at clinic reception

Outcomes

Higher weekly activity, stronger repeat visits, fewer no-shows, clearer program ROI

The Challenge

Communities

  • Members were active at events, then went quiet — one-off turnout rarely became ongoing engagement.
  • Managers struggled to personalize health content without a nutritionist or TCM team on staff.
  • Impact was hard to prove beyond raw attendance and social likes.

Chinese clinics

  • First visits were solid, but repeat visits and long-term follow-ups were inconsistent.
  • Staff spent real time on manual reminders, with low show-up rates in return.
  • In-person advice wasn't reinforced at home — which quietly eroded its perceived value.
Our approach

Five steps to lasting engagement

1) Frictionless onboarding

QR codes at events and at clinic reception link straight to the FoodTrack mini-app, with one-tap consent and plain-language data use and opt-outs.

2) FoodTrack's signature scan

A quick tongue scan runs FoodTrack's nutrition and TCM algorithm, returning a short profile — dryness, heat or cold tendencies, recovery status — with actionable tips on hydration, rest, food swaps, and gentle movement.

3) Personalized nudges & streaks

Weekly micro-goals for water, sleep, steps, and easy recipes are set from scan results, reinforced with streaks, gentle reminders, and optional micro-challenges like a 7-day recovery reset.

4) Lightweight CRM

Smart follow-ups — a "3-day check-in," a "14-day re-scan" — prevent no-shows through timed nudges and 1-tap rescheduling, while staff see who's slipping or due for a milestone.

5) Impact dashboards that matter

Communities see weekly active members, challenge completions, and sentiment. Clinics see repeat-visit rate, reactivations, no-show change, and NPS-style feedback — all exportable.

Outcome

Preliminary Results

Community pilot

Weekly active participation rose steadily after Week 3. Event-to-ongoing conversion improved once on-site scans were paired with tailored follow-ups, and sponsorship reporting became evidence-based with real engagement curves and completion rates.

TCM clinic pilot

Repeat visits rose after scan-anchored reminders with concrete next steps. No-shows fell with timed nudges and simple rescheduling, and perceived value rose as patients referred back to their scan and tips during follow-ups.

"The tongue scan gave our members a reason to come back every week."— Community Program Lead
"Patients arrived primed by the tips — consults and follow-ups felt more natural and less salesy."— Chief TCM Practitioner
