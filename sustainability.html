Our Sustainability Pillars

Growth that doesn't cost the planet

Commitment to Responsible AI

Digital transformation must go hand in hand with green transformation. We recognize the environmental footprint of large-scale AI, and we're committed to making AI drive sustainability rather than add to the risk.

Energy Efficiency First

We prioritize smaller, highly energy-efficient AI models — favoring small language models (SLMs) for edge applications like FoodTrack and our Education SaaS platform — so we can scale globally while meeting our ESG commitments.

Alignment with Global Climate Action

Through the HKSTP ecosystem, our sustainability efforts align with leading climate goals, including net-zero emissions by 2045 — reflected in our use of renewable energy for infrastructure and in AI-enabled sustainability reporting.
